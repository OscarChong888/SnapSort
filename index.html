The Problem

Understanding food labels can be difficult, especially when users need information quickly.

Food packaging contains nutritional values and ingredient lists, but consumers may not always understand unfamiliar ingredients or know what the information means.

?

Problem Statement

Many consumers find it difficult or time-consuming to understand food ingredients and nutritional information, which can lead to uninformed food choices.

02

Our Solution

Point. Scan. Understand.

Snap Sort provides a simpler way to access food product and nutrition information using AI-powered scanning and explanation.
